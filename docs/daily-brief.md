# Daily Brief

The daily brief should help an owner or team act, not dump a dashboard.

## Owner Brief

Include:

- What was handled
- What changed
- What is due today
- What is overdue
- What is blocked
- What is waiting
- What truly needs owner
- Recommended next leadership move
- Work that can safely wait

## Team Brief

Include:

- Today's priorities
- Newly assigned work
- Blockers
- Waiting items
- Due or overdue items
- Recently completed work

Briefs must be traceable to real task and event data.
