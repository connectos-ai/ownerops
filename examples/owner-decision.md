from the standard range to the premium range.

## Why This Needs Owner

- Meaningful financial authorization.
- Team can prepare options, but cannot approve the spend.
- Decision blocks next vendor step.

REQUESTED BY: Morgan

PRIORITY: High
DUE: 2026-01-06
OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: true
OWNER DECISION REASON: Meaningful financial authorization
