# Daily Checklist

Copy into a channel canvas or daily review thread. All names and examples are
fictional.

## Today

- [ ] Review `#ownerops-inbox` for new tasks.
- [ ] Confirm each new task has one owner or is marked `Unassigned`.
- [ ] Move ready work into `Ready` or `In Progress`.
- [ ] Check due-today work.
- [ ] Check overdue work.
- [ ] Review `Waiting` items and ask for needed updates.
- [ ] Review `Blocked` items and name who can unblock each one.
- [ ] Review `Needs Owner` items and reduce to smallest decision needed.
- [ ] Mark completed work `Done`.
- [ ] Carry forward unfinished priorities.

## Daily Brief Format

```text
OWNEROPS DAILY

Due today:
- TASK-001: Send updated proposal - owner: Casey

Blocked:
- TASK-004: Confirm room setup - waiting on vendor quote

Needs owner:
- TASK-006: Approve event budget range - decision needed by 3 PM

Completed:
- TASK-002: Schedule client walkthrough

Recommended next move:
- Delegate TASK-007 to Morgan and keep owner focused on budget decision.
```
